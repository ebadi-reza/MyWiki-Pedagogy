## WPI term codes
WPI runs seven-week terms. Codes like **A25**, **B25**, **C26**, **D26**, **E25** mean
a term (A–E) in an academic year. A–D are the four seven-week terms of the regular
year; E denotes summer sessions (E-I, E-II). [REVIEW] confirm the exact A–E → calendar
mapping you want recorded.

## Programs & requirements
- **MQP** — Major Qualifying Project. Senior capstone, ~3–4 courses' worth; required of
  all undergraduates. Reza has advised 16 MQPs (64 students).
- **IQP** — Interactive Qualifying Project. Society-oriented capstone, typically junior
  year; can be off-campus. Reza advised 6 IQPs (22 students), incl. a D25 Lausanne cohort.
- **OER** — Open Educational Resources.

## The AI tools
- **GPThermo** ([gpthermo.wpi.edu](https://gpthermo.wpi.edu)) — in-house GenAI thermodynamics tutor; a multi-agent
  GPT-4o system (tool + retrieval augmentation) with PyroMat-backed calculations for 20+
  working fluids. Adoption: 19 verified users (as of the candidate statement). Accuracy:
  95% on a 20-question benchmark vs. 15–25% for public LLMs ([[gpthermo-accuracy]]). Built
  on a $16.5k MTLC grant; maintained post-grant.
- **StudyGuideAI** ([studyguideai.app](https://studyguideai.app)) — student-facing platform: upload material →
  quizzes, summaries, flashcards. Adoption figure: 73 users (as of candidate statement).
- **AI Pedagogy website** ([aipedagogy.wpi.edu](https://aipedagogy.wpi.edu)) — Reza's
  faculty-facing resource hub for the specialist role. See [[ai-pedagogy]].
