# Educational Tools

Cross-cutting thread. The AI-powered tools Reza built as artifacts and, increasingly, as
platforms for the [[ai-pedagogy]] research program. Primarily instruments of
[[accessibility]].

## The tools

- **[[gpthermo]]** — AI-powered thermodynamics tutor (gpthermo.wpi.edu). A multi-agent
  **GPT-4o** system with tool + retrieval augmentation and PyroMat-backed property
  calculations for 20+ working fluids. [VALIDATED] **95%** accuracy on a 20-question
  benchmark vs. 15–25% for public LLMs ([[gpthermo-accuracy]]); **19 verified users** at
  institutions beyond WPI. Built on a $16.5k MTLC grant after two unfunded NSF proposals
  on the same theme; maintained after the grant.
- **[[studyguideai]]** — student-facing platform (studyguideai.app): upload course
  material → generated quizzes, summaries, and flashcards. [VALIDATED] 73 users. Its
  design was informed directly by GPThermo.

## Role as research platforms

[ASPIRATIONAL] Both tools are intended to serve as research platforms for the future
[[ai-pedagogy]] program on how engineering students interact with AI in domain-specific
contexts.
