# Overview — Engineering Education & Pedagogy Wiki

This wiki is organized around one spine: Reza's teaching philosophy, stated in his
candidate statement as three pillars — **accessibility**, **stress reduction**, and
**self-sufficiency**. Almost everything else is an expression of one or more pillars.
Read [[teaching-philosophy]] first; it is the center that the threads connect back to.

Provenance legend used throughout: **[CLAIM]** = Reza's own assertion · **[VALIDATED]**
= backed by external evidence · **[ASPIRATIONAL]** = future/pending.

## The three pillars (the spine)

- **[[accessibility]]** — making robust engineering content available outside the
  classroom, and cost-effective alternatives to expensive tools.
- **[[stress-reduction]]** — reducing exam/course anxiety while maintaining rigor.
  This pillar has the strongest evidence base (survey data, external evaluation, a
  peer-reviewed paper).
- **[[self-sufficiency]]** — developing students who can solve unfamiliar problems,
  including critical, judgment-based use of AI.

## Cross-cutting threads

- **[[ai-pedagogy]]** — Reza's AI Pedagogy Specialist role (WPI's first), the AI
  Pedagogy website, faculty workshops, and his emerging research program on AI
  verification and judgment. Where much of the [ASPIRATIONAL] future lives.
- **[[educational-tools]]** — GPThermo and StudyGuideAI as built artifacts: what they
  do, adoption numbers, and their role as research platforms. These serve
  [[accessibility]] and feed [[ai-pedagogy]].
- **[[scholarship-and-dissemination]]** — the ASEE publication record, invited talks,
  and the full grant history (funded and unfunded).

## How it fits together

- The **tools** are instruments of **accessibility** and, increasingly, platforms for
  the **ai-pedagogy** research program.
- The **AI verification cycle** sits in **self-sufficiency** but is also the seed of
  the future research program in **ai-pedagogy**.
- **Stress reduction** is the most mature thread: piloted in Heat Transfer (A22),
  expanded to Kinematics and Statics, validated by survey data and an external
  evaluation, published at ASEE 2023.
- **Scholarship-and-dissemination** cuts across all three pillars — most works map
  back to a pillar.
