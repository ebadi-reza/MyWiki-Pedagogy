# Evidence: MTLC External Evaluation (Stress Reduction)

**Provenance:** [VALIDATED] — independent external evaluation. **De-identified by
policy:** the evaluator is NOT named here, and no individual student data appears — only
the aggregate findings are publishable (see the Publication rules in `CLAUDE.md`).

## The findings
An external evaluation conducted by the **Morgan Teaching and Learning Center (MTLC)** in
Reza's **Kinematics course (C24)** found:
- Roughly **one quarter** of the students scored as **highly-to-extremely stressed** by
  typical academic activities, measured with a **validated survey scale**.
- **92%** of those highly-stressed students reported at the end of the course that
  **each of the stress-reduction strategies worked** (moderate-to-very-high positive
  impact).
- The practices **did not reduce rigor** — students characterized the assessments as
  genuinely challenging.

## Why it counts as evidence
It is an independent, third-party evaluation (not Reza's self-report) supporting both the
efficacy **and** the maintained rigor of the [[stress-reduction]] practices.

## Source & provenance
[[candidate-statement]] and [[teaching-portfolio]]. The figures originate in a support
**letter** the MTLC evaluator wrote for Reza's **NSF Engineering PLUS Alliance stEm PEER
Academy** application (see [[stem-peer-academy]]). The letter itself is private reference
in `raw/` and is **never reproduced or attributed** on a public page — only these
de-identified aggregate findings are published. The evaluation also noted Reza does not
"dumb down" content to reduce anxiety.

## Claims it supports
- Efficacy + maintained rigor of [[weekly-group-quizzes]], [[midterm-makeup-exams]],
