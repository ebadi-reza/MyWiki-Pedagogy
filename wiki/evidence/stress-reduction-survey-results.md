# Evidence: Stress-Reduction Survey Results

**Provenance:** [VALIDATED] — student survey data, reported in aggregate. Underlying
microdata stays in `raw/`; only aggregate percentages are published here.

This page holds **two distinct surveys**, kept separate by cohort and sample size: the
2023 ASEE study (n = 26) and the 2024 IEC poster (n = 47).

## Survey A — 2023 ASEE study
From the **Kinematics survey, C23** (n = **26**, ~**90%** of the class), reported in the
2023 ASEE paper (Ebadi & Sabuncu):
- **40.8%** average improvement in exam performance from the [[midterm-makeup-exams]]
  practice (SD **29.4%** — now confirmed against the source).
- **77%** agreed the [[weekly-group-quizzes]] helped them better understand the topic.
- **65.4%** both taught and learned from their partner during the quizzes (two-way
  discussion).
- **65.4%** agreed that teaching their area of weakness enhanced comprehension.
- **48%** of eligible students chose the "teach it" make-up option.
- **38.6%** agreed the open-ended course project helped them understand the topic
  *(lower figure the authors attribute to survey timing — the project runs late in the
  term)*.
- **65.3%** reported a better experience in Kinematics than in similar courses; **76.9%**
  would want other professors to adopt similar modules.

## Survey B — 2024 IEC poster
A separate Junior-level ME (Kinematics) survey, **n = 47, 100% participation**, reported
on the 2024 Innovative Education Conference poster ([[2024-ebadi-iec]]). Percentages
combine the top two response categories, as labeled.

**Module rated highly or extremely helpful:** [[weekly-group-quizzes]] **83%** (61.7%
"extremely"), [[midterm-makeup-exams]] **62%**, [[note-templates]] **47%**,
[[two-part-final-exams]] **38%**.

**Self-reported learning gains (good or great gain):** confidence doing kinematics
analysis **68%**, enthusiasm for engineering **66%**, confidence understanding the
material **60%**, interest in more engineering classes **53%**, comfort taking a quiz or
exam **28%**.

**Anxiety prevalence (motivation — highly/always true):** worry after an exam about
performance **55%**; think during exams they may fail **23%**; too worn out from
pre-exam worry to do their best **19%**.

## Context
The three stress-reduction practices were piloted in Heat Transfer (A22) and expanded to
Kinematics (C23, C24, C25) and Statics (B24), with survey data collected at each stage.
See [[stress-reduction]]. Positive feedback continued in later offerings.
