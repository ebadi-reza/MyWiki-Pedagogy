# Weekly Group Quizzes

Assessment practice serving the [[stress-reduction]] pillar.

## What it is
[CLAIM] A weekly quiz taken in **pairs**. Each week partners work collaboratively for
**30 minutes** on a problem summarizing the week's concepts; their **joint solution
earns the full quiz grade regardless of accuracy**, which removes the grade threat and
incentivizes genuine cooperation. The instructor gives feedback during the work and then
spends the **final 20 minutes** working the problem and explaining its nuances.

[CLAIM] The design intent: a semi-private, peer-driven setting lets students ask
"seemingly trivial" questions of a classmate without fear of judgment, so the source of
stress is removed while comprehension improves.

## Evidence
[VALIDATED] Anonymous survey in **Kinematics, C23** (n = 26, ~90% of the class):
- **77%** agreed the weekly quizzes helped them better understand the topic.
- **65.4%** indicated they *both* taught and learned from their partner during the
  quizzes — evidence of genuine two-way discussion.

A later Kinematics survey (2024 IEC, n = 47) found **83%** rated the group quizzes
**highly or extremely helpful** (61.7% "extremely") — the highest-rated of the modules.
See [[2024-ebadi-iec]] and [[stress-reduction-survey-results]].

## Where/when it ran
Piloted in Heat Transfer (A22); expanded to Kinematics (C23, C24, C25) and Statics (B24).
