# Project Advising as Teaching

Serves the [[self-sufficiency]] pillar — where, in Reza's words, the self-sufficiency
approach is "most fully expressed."

## Advising philosophy
[CLAIM] The projects belong to the students, made clear from the start of every advising
relationship. The advisor's role is to **provide structure while giving students the
freedom to define and achieve their own objectives** — the same "solve problems you have
not seen before" aim as the classroom.

## The record (de-identified)
[VALIDATED] Since joining WPI in 2021: **16 MQPs (64 students)** and **6 IQPs (22
students)**. Highlights (team, member, and sponsor names omitted by policy):
- Four MQPs submitted **provisional patent applications**.
- An interdisciplinary project (AY22–23) spanned **four engineering departments (MME,
  RBE, CS, ECE)** with 10 students.
- Advised the **SAE Aero Design** project for four consecutive years; teams raised
  **$10k+ annually** and received MASGC travel grants.
- An MQP partnering with a Doctor of Physical Therapy designed an MS assistive device
  that **won best design award at the CMSC conference**.
- Two MQPs earned **honorable mention at the WPI Project Showcase**; another (a
  bio-inspired drag-reduction project) earned **honorable mention at the Young Engineer
  Paper Contest (IMECE)** and reached **peer-reviewed publication** →
  [[2024-drag-reduction-imece]].
- **D25 Lausanne, Switzerland IQP cohort**: 22 students across six sponsored projects;
  all six sponsors praised the students' performance and professionalism.

[CLAIM] Reza began advising MQPs in his first year — before he was scheduled to — when a
student group asked him to serve as their alternative advisor.

## Related scholarship
Reza is a co-author on a large WPI **alumni study** ([[2024-capstone-asee]]) finding that
capstone/MQP experiences significantly build professional skills and **self-efficacy**,
and that self-efficacy largely drives career preparedness — institutional-level evidence
for the value of the capstone advising described here. *(A co-authored study, not Reza's
own; see the page for attribution.)*
