# Two-Part Final Exams

Assessment practice serving the [[stress-reduction]] pillar. Targets the anxiety
associated with the final exam.

## How it works
[CLAIM] The final is structured in two parts; if students have extra time during **Part
2**, they may **revise their Part 1 answers**. Students are **not told in advance** that
Part 1 can be revised — a design choice intended to preserve academic integrity while
still allowing authentic self-correction.

## Evidence
[VALIDATED] On the 2024 IEC survey (Kinematics, n = 47), **38%** of students rated the
two-part final (labeled the "Redo exam" on the poster) **highly or extremely helpful**.
See [[2024-ebadi-iec]] / [[stress-reduction-survey-results]].

[VALIDATED — external] It is also among the practices the external MTLC evaluation found
effective and rigor-preserving (see [[mtlc-external-evaluation]]). One student
(anonymized) credited the structure with helping them manage stress related to exams and
assignments.

## Where/when it ran
Part of the stress-reduction set piloted in Heat Transfer (A22) and used in Kinematics
and Statics.
