# Iterative Course Development

A cross-cutting teaching approach that underlies the practices in all three pillars.

## The method: "every course as a study"
[CLAIM] Reza describes an experimentalist approach to teaching drawn from his research
training: **observe** what is not working → **form a hypothesis** about why → **design an
intervention** → **measure** whether it helped → **refine and repeat**. As he puts it,
his first solution to a problem is rarely his best one. This is the engine behind the
stress-reduction practices, the [[note-templates]], and his course redesigns.

## Worked example: Advanced CAD (ES 3323)
[CLAIM] Advanced CAD was an early course that Reza substantially revamped across
successive offerings in response to student feedback — redesigning labs, lectures, and
the project (using three reference books), adjusting pacing for the seven-week term,
building in more checkpoints, explicitly inviting questions, eliminating redundant labs,
adding sample quizzes, and recording short timestamped instructional videos. [VALIDATED]
By the A23 offering, course-quality evaluations reached **4.67 and 4.86** across the two
sections. A refined lab instruction from a later offering is Appendix A.6 of the
[[teaching-portfolio]].
