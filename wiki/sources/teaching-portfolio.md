# Source: Teaching Portfolio

**Document:** Reza Ebadi — Teaching Portfolio (41 pp: a ~6-page reflective narrative +
appendices A.1–A.10). Private source in `raw/my-portfolio/`; this page is the public-safe
summary, **not** a reproduction.

## What it adds beyond the [[candidate-statement]]
Where the candidate statement is a highlights reel, the portfolio is a **reflective
growth narrative** — how the practices developed, what failed first, and what was
learned. Its throughline is an experimentalist method: treat "every course as a study"
(observe → hypothesize → intervene → measure → iterate). See
[[iterative-course-development]].

## Public-safe summary of contents
- **Motivation** — the stress-reduction work grew out of a growing awareness of student
  **mental-health challenges** at WPI. *(Specific circumstances in the source are omitted
  by policy; only the general motivation is published.)*
- **Stress reduction** — full mechanics and survey data for the three practices:
  [[weekly-group-quizzes]], [[midterm-makeup-exams]], [[two-part-final-exams]]. Piloted
  in Heat Transfer (A22); expanded to Kinematics (C23–C25) and Statics (B24).
- **Accessibility + self-sufficiency (treated together)** — the low-cost flow-viz setup
  (addressing an identified gap: no flow-viz experiments in Fluids/Eng. Exp./MQPs for
  2022–23), the AI tools [[gpthermo]]/[[studyguideai]], OER for CAD, and PMKS+.
- **Eng. Exp. (ME 3902)** — developed from scratch in A25; AI permitted for hardware
  code *syntax* with understanding checked at checkpoints, and a final project requiring
  each team to integrate a hardware component not covered in the course (find the docs,
  the protocol, make it work). See [[self-sufficiency]]. Sample "code-logic" exam is
  Appendix A.4.
- **Project advising** — see [[project-advising]].
- **Reflection & growth** — using student feedback to revamp courses
  ([[iterative-course-development]]); the note-template evolution ([[note-templates]]).
- **AI Pedagogy Specialist** — faculty/student AI-literacy surveys, AI-ethics and
  assignment-redesign workshops, advisory circles, the monthly **AI Pedagogy Lab**, and
  the AI Pedagogy website. See [[ai-pedagogy]].
- **Dissemination & recognition** — ASEE every year; the [[morgan-worcester-instructorship]];
  admission to the NSF Engineering PLUS Alliance stEm PEER Academy ([[stem-peer-academy]]).
