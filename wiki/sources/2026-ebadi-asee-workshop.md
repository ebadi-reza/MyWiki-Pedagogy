# Source: 2026 ASEE Workshop — "Building AI-Enhanced Educational Websites"

**Citation:** R. Ebadi, "Building AI-Enhanced Educational Websites: A Hands-On Workshop
for Engineering Educators," workshop at the *2026 ASEE Annual Conference & Exposition*
(Charlotte, NC). Faculty-development workshop; materials are a 19-slide hands-on deck.

## What it is
[CLAIM] A **150-minute, hands-on workshop** (four phases: Framing & Setup → Guided Build →
Publish → Extend) in which every participant leaves with a **live, published, university-
branded course website — with no prior coding required**. It operationalizes the AI
Pedagogy Specialist's **sieve/incubator** functions ([[ai-pedagogy]]): equipping faculty
to build their own educational resources.

## The method: "vibe coding"
[CLAIM] Faculty use an AI model (ChatGPT, or any comparable model) as a coding partner in
a loop: **Prompt** (describe in plain language) → **Generate** (AI writes the code) →
**Inspect** (read, tweak, test in the browser) → **Iterate** (refine by conversation).
The **Inspect** step is the point: it mirrors, at the faculty level, the same
AI-as-a-tool-you-verify stance behind the student [[ai-verification-cycle]] — use AI, but
review and understand its output rather than trust it blindly.

## What it teaches
- **Web basics:** `index.html` (structure), `css/styles.css` (style), `images/`/`files/`
  (assets), and why standard naming conventions matter (AI recognizes them; easier
  debugging; servers expect `index.html`).
- **University branding:** the **60-30-10 color rule** and a 3-step brand extraction
  (screenshot the homepage → inspect for exact hex codes → prompt the AI with both).
- **Publishing:** **GitHub Pages** (version control as an "undo button," cloud backup,
  automatic publishing to a live URL), with Netlify as a fallback.
- **Extending:** turning a single-course page into a multi-course hub with per-course pages.

## Responsible-design emphasis
[CLAIM] The workshop explicitly teaches **designing responsibly**: keep sensitive
information safe (**"don't publish sensitive data — APIs, student info — to GitHub"**),
document the workflow, and maintain ethical transparency. (A discipline that echoes this
wiki's own public-by-construction rules.)

## Worked examples
Reza's own sites — [[gpthermo]] (gpthermo.wpi.edu), [[studyguideai]] (studyguideai.app),
and the AI Pedagogy website (aipedagogy.wpi.edu) — are used as models.
