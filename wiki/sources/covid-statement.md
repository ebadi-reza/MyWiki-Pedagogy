# Source: COVID Statement

**Document:** Reza Ebadi — COVID Statement (1 p). Private source in `raw/my-portfolio/`;
this page is the public-safe summary. Nothing in it required stripping beyond the dossier
voice — no third-party names, student data, or figures.

## What it establishes: the pandemic origin of several practices
[CLAIM] Reza experienced the onset and peak of the pandemic as a **Lecturer at UNH**,
where he built a set of remote-teaching skills — teaching and communicating remotely,
recording experimental instructions, delivering synchronous and asynchronous lectures,
and supporting students at a distance. He kept the ones students continued to find
useful:
- **Recording/broadcasting lectures** and **posting lecture notes** (now core to
  [[accessibility]]).
- **Optional virtual (hybrid) office hours** — location-independent help, with
  screen-sharing for technical software such as SolidWorks.

[CLAIM] He frames the **most valuable** thing he learned as non-technical: being
emotionally closer to students, understanding their circumstances, and prioritizing
their health and wellbeing.

## Link to the stress-reduction motivation
[CLAIM] The statement dates the shift in his priorities to his **first year at WPI
(AY21-22)**, when the scale of student **mental-health challenges** reshaped his
priorities toward reducing course/exam anxiety — the origin of the [[stress-reduction]]
practices. *(Kept in general terms per the wiki's publication policy.)*
