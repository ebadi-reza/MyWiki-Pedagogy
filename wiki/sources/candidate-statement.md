# Source: Candidate Statement

**Document:** Reza Ebadi — Candidate Statement (7 pp), the highest-level narrative of
his engineering-education work. Private source in `raw/my-portfolio/`; this page is the
public-safe summary, **not** a reproduction. Self-assessment and dossier framing are
omitted by design (see the Publication rules in `CLAUDE.md`).

## What it establishes

The statement is organized around the three-pillar [[teaching-philosophy]]
(accessibility, stress reduction, self-sufficiency) and separates **teaching quality**
(delivery) from **teaching impact** (lasting effects mapped to the pillars). It is the
spine document that most other pages elaborate.

## Public-safe summary of contents

- **Teaching quality** — consistently strong course/instructor evaluations across ME
  and ES courses; high-impact practices (project-based, collaborative, service
  learning, undergraduate research); live problem-solving/live-demo teaching; iterative
  course development, including developing Eng. Exp. (ME 3902) from scratch in A25. See
  [[teaching-evaluations]].
- **Accessibility** — recorded lecture videos with tracked viewing metrics, CAD
  tutorial videos, [[note-templates]], OER for CAD (EMPOwER grant), cost-effective tool
  substitutes (PMKS+, web-based CAD), and the AI tools [[gpthermo]] and [[studyguideai]].
- **Stress reduction** — three assessment practices ([[weekly-group-quizzes]],
  [[midterm-makeup-exams]], [[two-part-final-exams]]) piloted in Heat Transfer (A22),
  expanded to Kinematics and Statics, with survey data and an external MTLC evaluation.
  See [[stress-reduction]].
- **Self-sufficiency** — critical use of professional tools; the generative-AI approach
  culminating in the [[ai-verification-cycle]] (Kinematics, C26).
- **Project advising** — 16 MQPs (64 students) and 6 IQPs (22 students) since 2021,
  including a D25 Lausanne IQP cohort and four MQPs that filed provisional patents.
- **Scholarship & dissemination** — ASEE every year since joining WPI; invited talk at
  the Innovative Education Conference (URI); the grant record (funded internal +
  external proposals). See [[scholarship-and-dissemination]].
- **Service** — MME transfer-student course reviewer (since Summer 2023); committees on
  teaching/research faculty load and faculty compensation; a WPI–AUA AI-ethics
  committee; faculty advising roles.
- **Future plans** — [ASPIRATIONAL] a research program on **AI verification and
  judgment** in engineering education, using his courses as the laboratory and the
  tools as research platforms; scaling the AI Pedagogy Specialist role institutionally.
